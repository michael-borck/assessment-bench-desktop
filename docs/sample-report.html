Data-Viz Assignment 2 — 24 submissions

3 arms · 5 repeated runs per LLM arm · marks out of 100 · compared against the lecturer's marks

The bench measures how each method agrees with the human marks. It never marks students.

What this run found

  • “hybrid-claude” showed strong agreement with your marks (r=0.87 across 24 submissions) and varied ±2.3 points between repeated runs.
  • “llm-claude” showed strong agreement with your marks (r=0.81 across 24 submissions) and varied ±5.6 points between repeated runs.
  • “llm-local” (Ollama, llama3.2) showed moderate agreement with your marks (r=0.58 across 24 submissions) and varied ±11.2 points between repeated runs.
  • The single signal that best tracked your marks was document.word_count (r=0.71) — moderate agreement without any LLM.
  • Submissions typical of the cohort tended to receive higher marks (similarity vs marks r=0.44) — worth a look at how the distinctive ones were marked.
